In high school, I think I'm very gifted when it comes to learning. Now in my 30s, I know I'm a terrible learner. The thing is if you discover you need to study hard to grasp something it is double hard for you. Just because you were good at school it doesn't mean you can postpone to study or it takes very short amount of time. That's basically my case, and I hope I can break this with the help of this blog. This post explains why I'm in this situation, and hopefully I can figure out some patterns and find some anti patterns while I'm writing

#### 1. Jack of All Trades, Master of None!

Since I was a little kid, I wanted to be everything! If I saw a masterpiece of a guitar solo, I was just jealous, and I wanted to become as good as that musician. If I saw a cool and successful person in any kind of field, I was instantly wanted to be good as that person. It has both pros and cons.
