In high school, I think I'm very gifted when it comes to learning. Now in my 30s, I know I'm a terrible learner. The thing is if you discover you need to study hard to grasp something it is double hard for you. Just because you were good at school it doesn't mean you can postpone to study or it takes very short amount of time. That's basically my case, and I hope I can break this with the help of this blog. This post explains why I'm in this situation, and hopefully I can figure out some patterns and find some anti patterns while I'm writing

### 1. Jack of All Trades, Master of None!

Since I was a little kid, I wanted to be everything! If I saw a masterpiece of a guitar solo, I was just jealous, and I wanted to become as good as that musician. If I saw a cool and successful person in any kind of field, I was instantly wanted to be good as that person. I think it comes with both upsides and downsides. On one hand, it quickly broadening your interests, and fill you with lots of inspiration on so many subjects. Also, people think you are really smart! In fact, you just know some basics of the subject, and sometimes you know how things work generally in this subject. But it comes with a great danger. Not just other people, also you can think that you are really really smart. But most of the time, it's not true. One day I wanted be and felt so motivated to become an artist, one day writer, one day engineer. Today, I'm not even sure if I'm a good engineer -probably not- or I should have already work on different paths. Regardless of the path I choose, main issue is still couldn't stick on a fixed path.
